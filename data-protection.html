Data Protection Compliance (GDPR & India DPDP Act, 2023)

Effective Date: March 1, 2026 | Entity: PurnOrganic

PurnOrganic is committed to complying with applicable data protection laws, including:

  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA)
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for users in India

1. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent – when you explicitly agree (e.g., marketing communications)
  • Contractual Necessity – to fulfil orders or provide platform services
  • Legal Obligation – to comply with applicable laws
  • Legitimate Interest – to improve services, prevent fraud, and ensure platform security

2. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are an EEA user, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data (“Right to be Forgotten”)
  • Restrict Processing of your data
  • Data Portability (receive your data in a structured format)
  • Object to processing, including profiling or direct marketing
  • Withdraw Consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: support@purnorganic.com

3. Your Rights Under India DPDP Act, 2023

If you are a user in India, you are entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to Access Information about your personal data
  • Right to Correction and Erasure of personal data
  • Right to Grievance Redressal
  • Right to Nominate another individual to exercise rights in case of incapacity or death

4. Consent Management

  • We obtain clear and informed consent before collecting personal data
  • Users can withdraw consent at any time
  • Withdrawal of consent may affect access to certain platform features

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary:

  • To fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy
  • To comply with legal, tax, or regulatory requirements
  • To resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Once no longer required, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Data Transfers (Cross-Border)

Your data may be stored or processed outside your country of residence.

  • For GDPR users: We ensure appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • For Indian users: We comply with DPDP Act provisions on cross-border data transfer, as notified by authorities

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach:

  • Affected users will be notified promptly
  • Relevant authorities will be informed as required under applicable law
  • Necessary corrective measures will be taken immediately

8. Children’s Data (Enhanced Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 without verifiable parental consent, in compliance with:

  • GDPR Article 8
  • DPDP Act provisions

9. Complaints & Supervisory Authorities

  • GDPR users can lodge complaints with their local Data Protection Authority (DPA)
  • Indian users can approach the Data Protection Board of India (as notified under DPDP Act)
