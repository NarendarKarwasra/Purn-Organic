Supplier Onboarding Agreement

Effective Date: March 1, 2026 | Entity: PurnOrganic

This Agreement governs the relationship between PurnOrganic (“Company”) and the Supplier (“Supplier”).

1. Nature of Relationship

  • Supplier provides products to PurnOrganic

  • PurnOrganic may:

    • Purchase
    • Brand
    • Distribute
    • Resell

2. Scope of Engagement

Supplier agrees to:

  • Supply agreed products
  • Maintain quality standards
  • Fulfill supply commitments

3. Product Standards

Supplier must ensure:

  • Products are safe, authentic, and of agreed quality
  • No adulteration or misrepresentation

4. Quality Assurance

PurnOrganic may:

  • Inspect supplier facilities
  • Test product samples
  • Reject non-compliant goods

5. Packaging

Supplier must:

  • Use appropriate packaging for safe transport
  • Ensure product integrity

6. Pricing

  • Pricing mutually agreed
  • Subject to market conditions

7. Order Fulfilment

Supplier must:

  • Deliver on time
  • Maintain consistency
  • Inform delays proactively

8. Payments & Settlement

A. Payment Mode

  • Bank transfer / UPI / NEFT / IMPS

B. Settlement Cycle

  • Same-day settlement (T+0) upon:

    • Delivery confirmation
    • Internal verification

C. Advance & Adjustments

  • Advance payments may be issued where applicable

  • Adjustments may include:

    • Returns
    • Quality issues
    • Logistics costs

9. Returns & Liability

Supplier is responsible for:

  • Defective or damaged goods
  • Quality complaints

PurnOrganic may deduct losses from payouts.

10. Compliance

Supplier must comply with:

  • Applicable laws and regulations
  • Tax requirements (GST, if applicable)

11. Prohibited Conduct

Supplier must NOT:

  • Provide false claims
  • Supply compromised products
  • Circumvent payment systems

12. Intellectual Property

  • PurnOrganic retains rights over brand and packaging
  • Supplier cannot use branding without permission

13. Indemnity

Supplier indemnifies PurnOrganic against:

  • Product-related claims
  • Legal violations

14. Termination

Immediate termination in case of:

  • Quality failure
  • Fraud
  • Repeated complaints

15. Force Majeure

No liability for uncontrollable disruptions.

16. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

  • Laws of India
  • Jurisdiction: Noida
