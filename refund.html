Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: March 10, 2026 | Entity: PurnOrganic

1. Nature of Products

PurnOrganic primarily deals in:

  • Fresh organic produce
  • Farm-based products
  • Agricultural services and learning programs

Due to the perishable nature of products, refunds and returns are handled carefully.

2. Order Cancellation

Before Dispatch

  • Orders can be cancelled before shipment for a full refund

After Dispatch

  • Cancellation may not be possible once the product is shipped

3. Returns & Refunds (Products)

You may be eligible for a refund/replacement if:

  • Product is damaged or spoiled on delivery
  • Wrong product delivered
  • Significant quality issues

Conditions

  • Issue must be reported within 24 hours of delivery
  • Supporting proof (images/videos) must be provided
  • Product should not be excessively used

4. Non-Returnable Items

  • Perishable goods after 24 hours
  • Products consumed or used beyond reasonable inspection
  • Items marked as non-returnable

5. Refund Process

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 7–14 business days
  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method

6. Failed Transactions

  • If payment is deducted but order is not confirmed, refund will be processed automatically within 7–14 business days

7. Platform Role

PurnOrganic may assist in dispute resolution but:

  • Final resolution may depend on seller/farmer policies
  • We aim to ensure fair outcomes for all parties

8. Contact for Refunds

Email: support@purnorganic.com

Include:

  • Order ID
  • Issue description
  • Supporting images/videos
