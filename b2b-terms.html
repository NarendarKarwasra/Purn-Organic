B2B Wholesale Terms

Orders may be placed via:

C. Order Confirmation

Orders are confirmed only after:

Taxes

Applicable taxes (including GST) will be charged as per law

A. Payment Structure

B. Credit Period

  • Delay in payment may result in:

    • Suspension of future orders
    • Additional charges (if applicable)

Payments may be processed via:

  • Bank transfer

A. Delivery Timelines
