Seller Agreement (Farmer / Vendor Onboarding Agreement)

Effective Date: April 2, 2026 | Entity: PurnOrganic

1. Parties

This agreement is between:

  • PurnOrganic (Platform Provider)
  • Seller / Farmer / Vendor ("Seller")

2. Scope of Services

PurnOrganic provides:

  • Marketplace listing
  • Order management
  • Payment collection and payout facilitation
  • Logistics integration

3. Seller Obligations

The Seller agrees to:

A. Product Integrity

  • Sell genuine, organic, and high-quality products
  • Ensure compliance with applicable agricultural and food safety laws
  • Avoid false or misleading claims

B. Inventory & Fulfilment

  • Maintain accurate inventory
  • Fulfil orders within defined timelines
  • Ensure proper packaging (especially for perishable goods)

C. Pricing & Transparency

  • Provide accurate pricing and product descriptions
  • Clearly mention quantity, quality grade, and origin

D. Compliance

Comply with:

  • Local agricultural and trade laws
  • Tax regulations (GST, if applicable)

4. Payments & Settlements

  • Payments will be processed via PurnOrganic payout system

  • Seller agrees to:

    • Settlement timelines (T+0 days)
    • Platform commission/fees
  • Payouts will be made to verified bank accounts only

5. Returns, Refunds & Disputes

  • Seller agrees to honor platform refund policies

  • In case of:

    • Damaged goods
    • Incorrect delivery
    • Quality complaints

  • PurnOrganic may:

    • Issue refunds
    • Adjust seller payouts

6. Prohibited Conduct

Seller shall NOT:

  • Sell counterfeit or non-organic products under false claims
  • Manipulate pricing or listings
  • Circumvent platform payments (no direct deals outside platform)

7. Suspension & Termination

PurnOrganic reserves the right to:

  • Suspend listings
  • Withhold payouts
  • Terminate seller account

In case of:

  • Fraud
  • Repeated complaints
  • Policy violations

8. Liability

  • Seller is solely responsible for:

    • Product quality
    • Compliance with laws
  • PurnOrganic acts only as a platform

9. Indemnity

Seller agrees to indemnify PurnOrganic against:

  • Claims arising from product issues
  • Regulatory violations
  • Customer disputes

10. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India, jurisdiction: Noida
