Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026 | Entity: PurnOrganic

PurnOrganic.com (“PurnOrganic”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to safeguarding your personal data and ensuring transparency in how your information is collected, used, processed, stored, and shared.

PurnOrganic operates as a consumer-focused brand engaged in sourcing, processing, packaging, distributing, and selling organic and natural products directly to individual customers and businesses (B2B) across India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you interact with our platform.

Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all interactions with PurnOrganic, including but not limited to.

  • Our website: www.PurnOrganic.com
  • Mobile applications
  • Customer support channels
  • Order placement, delivery, and payment processes
  • Supplier/vendor onboarding and management
  • B2B wholesale transactions and partnerships
  • Marketing campaigns, surveys, and promotions

This policy applies whether you are:

  • A retail customer
  • A business customer
  • A supplier, farmer, or vendor

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information to provide and improve our services, including:

A. Information You Provide

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Delivery and billing address
  • Account login details
  • Preferences related to organic products, farmers, or services
  • Any information submitted during onboarding as a buyer, farmer, or partner

You will always be informed about which information is mandatory and which is optional.

B. Transaction & Usage Information

  • Order history and purchase behaviour
  • Payment details (processed securely via payment gateways)
  • Interactions with farmers, or platform features

C. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths
  • Cookies and usage behaviour

This data helps us improve platform performance, user experience, and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and manage services on PurnOrganic
  • To process orders and facilitate payments
  • To connect users with farmers, and institutions
  • To personalize your experience
  • To improve platform functionality and offerings
  • To communicate updates, offers, and important notifications
  • To detect fraud and ensure platform security

We process your information:

  • With your consent
  • To fulfil contractual obligations
  • To comply with legal requirements
  • For legitimate business interests

3. User Roles & Platform Access

PurnOrganic enables multiple user types including:

  • Buyers / Consumers
  • Farmers / Producers
  • Institutions / Wholesalers

Each user type may provide additional information relevant to their role, which will be used strictly to enable platform functionality and connections.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember user preferences
  • Improve navigation and experience
  • Analyse traffic and behaviour
  • Provide relevant content and offers

You can disable cookies via your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share your information with:

A. Service Providers

  • Payment gateways
  • Logistics/delivery partners
  • Technology and analytics providers

B. Platform Partners

If you engage with farmers, or institutions, relevant information may be shared to enable the service.

All partners are required to maintain confidentiality and comply with applicable data protection laws.

6. Payment & Financial Information

  • We do not store sensitive payment details like card numbers
  • Payments are processed through secure, encrypted third-party gateways
  • Financial data is used only to complete transactions

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable:

  • Technical safeguards
  • Organizational controls
  • Security practices

However, no system is 100% secure. If you suspect a security issue, please contact us immediately.

8. Children’s Privacy

PurnOrganic is not intended for users under 18 years of age. Minors may use the platform only under parental or guardian supervision.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Update or correct information
  • Request deletion (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and continued use of the platform implies acceptance of the updated terms.
